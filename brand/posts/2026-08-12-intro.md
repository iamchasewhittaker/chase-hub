First post here, so an intro.

I spent 7 years getting merchants live on Authorize.Net and CyberSource. Payment flows, integrations, the calls where something breaks two days before launch and you have to figure out why. Over 200 merchants live. Most of what I learned wasn't in the docs.

This year I started building software with AI tools. Not "playing with AI." Shipping. Real apps against payment-style APIs, with the same instinct I used on go-live calls: what breaks first, and why.

This feed is where the two meet. I write about payments and fintech news from an operator's seat, the part press releases skip. I write about what's actually true when you build with AI, not the hype version. One real post at a time, no filler.

If you've been on either side of a merchant go-live, or you're building with AI and hitting the parts nobody warns you about, I'd like to hear what you've seen.
