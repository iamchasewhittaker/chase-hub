# Sources — where to pull articles

You already receive most of what you need. Skim, pick one, react.

## In your inbox (newsletters)
- TLDR (dan@tldrnewsletter.com) — daily tech, usually a fintech or payments item.
- The Batch / DeepLearning.AI — AI developments.
- Farnam Street — thinking and mental models (good for the occasional non-news post).

## Payments + fintech
- American Banker (payments section)
- PaymentsDive, Finextra, The Paypers
- Visa / Stripe / Adyen / Mastercard newsrooms (primary sources)
- Digital Commerce 360 (merchant side)

## AI + building
- The Batch, Axios AI, TLDR AI
- Anthropic and OpenAI blogs (primary sources)

## How to pick
One story that touches BOTH payments and AI is ideal. That's your seat. If it's payments-only or AI-only, find the operator angle the headline skips. If you have no real angle, skip it. Don't post filler.
