# Brand Playbook — payments operator + AI builder

> Clock 1 (money-now) comes first. This brand is the long game and must never displace the July floor or the Citcon cover letter. A few small posts a week, shipped honest. Not a content empire.

## The angle (the one thing to be known for)
A payments operator who now builds with AI. Seven years getting merchants live at Authorize.Net and CyberSource, now shipping real software with AI tools. You analyze payments, fintech, and AI from an operator's seat, the part the press releases skip. Very few people can credibly stand here.

## The model: write once, distribute three places
- **Own it:** post to the chase-hub feed first. That is the asset you own. No algorithm can take it.
- **Distribute:** cross-post to LinkedIn and Threads.
- The owned post is the floor signal (see STATE.md, metric `chase_hub_posts`).

## The floor
**3 posts a week.** Plus comment on other people's posts more than you post; engagement compounds faster than posting alone. The tracked minimum is 1 owned post per week. Aim for 3.

## The format: the article reaction
1. **Hook** — one honest line. Your read, not clickbait.
2. **What it is** — one or two plain lines on the article or news.
3. **Your operator take** — the value. What most people miss, grounded in something you actually saw. "In 7 years getting merchants live, I watched..."
4. **So what** — what it means for operators or builders reading.
5. **One real question** to invite replies. Two or three plain hashtags.

Length ~150–250 words. Short paragraphs, one idea each.

## Voice rules (non-negotiable)
- No em-dashes.
- No rule-of-threes (no rhetorical triads).
- No hype words (amazing, incredible, transformative, thrilled).
- No consultant phrasing (synergy, passion, leverage as a verb).
- Short sentences. Concrete numbers. Real questions. Say "I don't know" when it's true.
- The test: read it aloud. If it sounds like someone trying hard on LinkedIn, rewrite it. If it sounds like Chase at 4pm on a Friday call with a merchant, ship it.

## Workflow (built for low vision)
Talk your take into Dictation (Fn Fn). Let Claude shape the draft. You edit it to your voice. Your eyes never have to grind through long typing.

## Anti-avoidance rule
Ship at "good and honest," not perfect. Three ugly-but-true posts beat one polished post that never goes out. Do NOT build tooling (scheduler, analytics, linter) until the posting habit is real. The habit first. The system maybe, later.
